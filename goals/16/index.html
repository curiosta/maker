Sustainable Development Goal 16

Peace, Justice and Strong Institutions

This goal aims for peaceful, inclusive societies with accountable institutions and participatory decision-making. Makers can explore designing for trust, transparency and fairness in small group processes such as club or classroom votes.

2 projects for SDG 16 · Peace, Justice and Strong Institutions

  • intermediate
  • 8-10 hours

Tamper-Evident Electronic Vote Box for Clubs and Classrooms

Explores how to design for trust in group decisions: one vote per voter, a tamper flag and an auditable log, in the spirit of participatory decision-making (target 16.7).

Tamper-Detecting Smart Seal (ATtiny, Cast in Epoxy)

A low-cost seal that records if and when it was broken explores how technology can support accountability and evidence integrity (targets 16.5 and 16.6).
