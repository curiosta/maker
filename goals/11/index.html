Sustainable Development Goal 11

Sustainable Cities and Communities

This goal is about cities and communities that are inclusive, safe, resilient and sustainable, including lower environmental impact such as air quality and noise. Makers can build sensors that map local conditions and give neighbourhoods data to act on.
