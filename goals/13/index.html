Sustainable Development Goal 13

Climate Action

Climate Action covers urgent action on climate change and its impacts, including adaptation and awareness. Makers can build monitors that log local conditions such as heat and humidity over time, making local climate trends visible.

ESP32 Climate Node: Heat-Index Monitor with OLED and MQTT Dashboard

Logs local temperature, humidity and heat index over time and flags heat-stress hours, turning a weather node into a small climate-awareness and adaptation tool.
