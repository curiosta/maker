Sustainable Development Goal 14

Life Below Water

Life Below Water is about conserving oceans, seas and marine resources and reducing pollution that reaches them. Makers can build sensing buoys and loggers for lakes, rivers and coasts, testing carefully and leaving nothing behind in the water.

LoRa Water-Quality Buoy (Temperature + Turbidity)

Lets students monitor temperature and turbidity in a lake, backwater or harbour edge, the kind of local data that supports reducing water pollution (target 14.1).
