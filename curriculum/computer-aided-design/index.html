What the official assignment asks for

2D and 3D design tools, parametric and programmatic design, simulation, rendering and interchange formats.

Individual assignment

  • Model (raster, vector, 2D, 3D, render, animate, simulate, ...) a possible final project.
  • Compress your images and videos.
  • Post a description with your design files on your class page.

How it is assessed

Paraphrased

From the Fab Academy 2026 assessment page on fabacademy.org, retrieved 29 Oct 2026. Your local and global instructors decide what passes.

Learning outcomes

  • Evaluate and select 2D and 3D software.
  • Demonstrate and describe the processes you used to model in 2D and 3D.
  • Demonstrate image and video compression.

Clarified in the official FAQ

  • Model a possible final project, documented in at least 2D and 3D.
  • Parametric design is not compulsory this week, but it is in computer-controlled cutting.

All modules also follow the general essentials (original files in your archive, compressed media, acknowledgements, AI prompts documented) and the commercial board policy. Summary of both.

Documentation checklist

The official "have you answered these questions?" list for this module, paraphrased. Tick each one off in your documentation.

  • Modelled experimental objects or part of a possible final project in 2D and/or 3D.
  • Shown how you did it, with words, images or screenshots.
  • Documented how you compressed your images and video.
  • Included your original design files.

Bare-minimum vs exceptional

Author's interpretation

Author's interpretation, not official Fab Academy grading. Use it to aim your work and check your documentation, and confirm what counts with your instructor.

Bare-minimum

  • A 2D drawing and a 3D model of part of your final project, with the steps shown.
  • The original files (not only exports or screenshots) in your repository.
  • The commands or settings you used to compress images and video, with before and after sizes.

Exceptional

  • Several tools tried and compared honestly for your project (for example a parametric modeller, a mesh tool and a code-based modeller).
  • A parametric model you can resize by changing a few variables.
  • A render, animation or simple simulation that answers a real design question.
  • Interchange formats tested, for example exporting to STEP or STL and reopening the file elsewhere.

Common pitfalls

The author's notes, partly drawn from the official FAQ. Not official grading.

  • Screenshots only, with no source files.
  • Exporting only STL, which loses the editable history.
  • Forgetting the compression part of the assignment.

Open tools and references

  • FreeCADopen parametric 3D CAD
  • FreeCAD Sketcherconstraint-based 2D sketches
  • OpenSCADcode-based 3D modelling
  • CadQueryPython parametric CAD
  • SolveSpacesmall parametric CAD
  • Blendermesh modelling, rendering, animation
  • Inkscape (source and releases)open 2D vector drawing
  • GIMPraster image editing
  • ImageMagickbatch-resize and compress images
  • FFmpegcompress and trim video
  • HandBrakevideo compression with a GUI
