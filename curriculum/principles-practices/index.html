Documentation checklist

The official "have you answered these questions?" list for this module, paraphrased. Tick each one off in your documentation.

  • A sketch of your final project idea or ideas.
  • A short description of what it will do and who will use it.
  • A page on your site with the sketch and description.

Bare-minimum vs exceptional

Author's interpretation

Author's interpretation, not official Fab Academy grading. Use it to aim your work and check your documentation, and confirm what counts with your instructor.

Bare-minimum

  • At least one sketch of a possible final project, with a paragraph on what it does and who it is for.
  • The sketch and description live on a final project page of your class site.
  • You have read the Fab Charter and the student agreement, and you know where the assessment guide is.

Exceptional

  • Two or three candidate ideas compared, with the reasons you chose one.
  • A first look at prior work: similar projects in the Fab Academy archive, linked and credited.
  • A rough map of which modules the project will use, so later weeks can feed into it.
  • An early list of open questions and risks you want to answer first.

Common pitfalls

The author's notes, partly drawn from the official FAQ. Not official grading.

  • Picking a project that depends on one hard step you have never tried. Name that step early.
  • A sketch with no words: say what it does and who uses it.
  • Copying an archive project without credit. Link and acknowledge what inspired you.
