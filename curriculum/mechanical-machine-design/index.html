What the official assignment asks for

Two class pages share this lecture date: mechanical design (build the machine's mechanical parts) and machine design (actuate and automate it). Both are group assignments.

Group assignment

  • Design a machine that includes mechanism, actuation, automation, function and user interface.
  • Build the mechanical parts and operate it manually.
  • Actuate and automate your machine.
  • Document the group project and your individual contribution.
  • Prepare a demonstration of your machines for the next class.

How it is assessed

Paraphrased

From the Fab Academy 2026 assessment page on fabacademy.org, retrieved 29 Oct 2026. Your local and global instructors decide what passes.

Learning outcomes

  • Work and communicate effectively as a team.
  • Design, plan and build a machine.
  • Analyse and solve technical problems.
  • Recognise opportunities to improve the design.

Clarified in the official FAQ

  • A group of three to five is suggested, and each member should contribute about a week's work.
  • Working alone is not allowed.
  • Commercial boards and off-the-shelf software may be used for the machine.

All modules also follow the general essentials (original files in your archive, compressed media, acknowledgements, AI prompts documented) and the commercial board policy. Summary of both.

Documentation checklist

The official "have you answered these questions?" list for this module, paraphrased. Tick each one off in your documentation.

  • The machine building process documented on the group page.
  • Your individual contribution documented on your own site.
  • Links from your page to the group page and back.
  • How the team planned, shared out tasks and carried out the project (group page).
  • Problems and how the team solved them (group page).
  • Possible improvements (group page).
  • Design files (group page).
  • The machine presented globally and/or a 1-minute video (1920x1080 MP4) and a slide (1920x1080 PNG) on the group page.

Bare-minimum vs exceptional

Author's interpretation

Author's interpretation, not official Fab Academy grading. Use it to aim your work and check your documentation, and confirm what counts with your instructor.

Bare-minimum

  • A working machine with mechanism, actuation, automation and a user interface, first operated by hand and then automated.
  • A group page with planning, task split, problems, improvements, files, slide and video.
  • Your own page saying clearly what you did, with links both ways.

Exceptional

  • A modular design others can rebuild, with a bill of materials and assembly steps.
  • Motion tested and tuned (backlash, accuracy, speed), with measurements.
  • A clear system diagram of mechanics, electronics and software.
  • A short honest review of what the team would change in a second version.

Common pitfalls

The author's notes, partly drawn from the official FAQ. Not official grading.

  • One person doing all the work while others document.
  • Starting with electronics and software before the mechanism moves by hand.
  • Missing the video and slide.
  • Vague individual pages that do not say who did what.
