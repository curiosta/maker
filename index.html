What is here

  • Projects. 5 ideas from beginner to advanced, each with what it teaches, a build time, safety notes and a priced parts list.
  • Parts catalogue. 38 priced parts used by the projects, with the seller's price, a source link and the date it was read. Parts shared between projects are marked. There are also 26 draft parts whose prices have not been researched yet; they are listed separately and are in no project or total.
  • Curriculum. An unofficial companion to the Fab Academy curriculum: what each module's official assignment asks for, and what bare-minimum and exceptional work could look like. Open the curriculum.
  • Ask. An optional tutor that runs in your browser with your own OpenRouter account. About the tutor.
  • Buying. There is no online checkout. Email info@curiosta.com to buy or import parts.

How we show prices

  • Prices are what a seller listed, including GST, on the date shown next to each part. They are not a Curiosta quote.
  • No listing states a GST rate. Every part is marked GST unverified, typically 18%. Confirm the rate on a seller invoice.
  • Where we have not found a price, the page says price not available. We do not guess.
  • Shipping is not included. Stock and prices change.
