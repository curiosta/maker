Sensor.Community Air-Quality Node (Join a Global Open-Data Network)

  • beginner
  • 3-5 hours

SDG 17 · Partnerships for the Goals

Your node joins Sensor.Community, a volunteer citizen-science network that shares air-quality data openly across many countries: a working example of multi-stakeholder data partnerships.

Safety notes

  • Mount outdoors 1.5-3.5 m high, out of rain, using a certified 5 V USB adapter; keep the adapter indoors or in a weatherproof box.
  • The node publishes a rounded location; register with an address you are comfortable sharing.

Draft and unofficial, not affiliated with Fab Academy or the United Nations. Check prices, GST and parts before you buy.

Summary

Build the airRohr PM2.5/PM10 station (NodeMCU ESP8266 + SDS011 + BME280) in a two-elbow PVC weather shield, following the Sensor.Community guide, and put your street on the public map.

What it teaches

Flash community firmware, wire a particulate sensor and an environment sensor to an existing open spec, register a node, and work to someone else's standard so your data is comparable. Compare your readings with the nearest official monitoring station and document the build for others.

Topics it touches, in Fab Academy-style terms:

  • Input devices
  • Networking and communications
  • Embedded programming
  • Project development (documentation)
