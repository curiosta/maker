BLE SOS Pendant with Loud Alarm (3D-Printed, XIAO ESP32-C3)

  • intermediate
  • 8-10 hours

SDG 5 · Gender Equality

A user-tested personal-safety alarm prototype responds to SDG target 5.2 on ending violence against women and girls in public and private spaces.

Safety notes

Read first. Prototype only. It does not replace India's 112 emergency service or the 112 India app, and it stores no location data.

  • LiPo: use a cell with a protection circuit, check polarity before connecting to the XIAO BAT pads, never puncture, crush or short it, and stop using a swollen cell.
  • Prototype only: it does not replace India's 112 emergency service or the 112 India app. Make that clear to anyone who tests it.
  • The alarm is loud: test at arm's length, not next to your ear.

Draft and unofficial, not affiliated with Fab Academy or the United Nations. Check prices, GST and parts before you buy.

Summary

A long press sounds a loud alarm and sends a BLE alert to a paired phone page, which can show a pre-written message to share. Built with and tested by the people who will carry it; stores no location data.

What it teaches

Design a small 3D-printed enclosure with a recessed button that is hard to press by accident, drive a loud buzzer through a MOSFET, use BLE notifications to tell a paired phone (Web Bluetooth page), and charge a LiPo through the XIAO's battery pads while staying in low-power sleep.

Topics it touches, in Fab Academy-style terms:

  • Computer-aided design
  • 3D scanning and printing
  • Electronics design
  • Electronics production
  • Output devices
  • Networking and communications
