Clip-On Machine Utilisation Logger (Industry 4.0 Retrofit)

  • intermediate
  • 8-10 hours

SDG 9 · Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure

Gives small workshops affordable insight into how much their existing machines actually run, upgrading old equipment without replacing it (targets 9.2 and 9.3).

Safety notes

  • Mount only on stationary, external surfaces away from moving parts, belts, chucks and swarf; never mount on a spindle or guard that moves.
  • Do not connect to machine wiring or mains; this design is vibration-only by intent.
  • Tell operators what is measured; it logs machine state, not people.

Draft and unofficial, not affiliated with Fab Academy or the United Nations. Check prices, GST and parts before you buy.

Summary

Stick it on a lathe, drill press or sewing machine: it detects when the machine is running from vibration and reports hours, starts and idle gaps per shift. No electrical connection to the machine at all.

What it teaches

Mill a small board for an accelerometer node, mount it with magnets on a machine body, classify running vs idle from vibration energy, and publish run-time and cycle counts over MQTT to a shift dashboard without touching the machine's wiring.

Topics it touches, in Fab Academy-style terms:

  • Electronics design
  • Electronics production
  • Input devices
  • Networking and communications
  • Interface and application programming
