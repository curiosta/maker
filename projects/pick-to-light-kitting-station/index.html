Pick-to-Light Kitting Station for Small Workshops

  • beginner
  • 5-7 hours

SDG 8 · Decent Work and Economic Growth

Simple, affordable automation raises productivity and cuts errors for micro-manufacturers and home-based assembly work (target 8.2).

Safety notes

  • Laser cutter: never leave it running unattended, keep the lid closed and extraction on, and only cut materials your lab approves (no PVC or vinyl).

Draft and unofficial, not affiliated with Fab Academy or the United Nations. Check prices, GST and parts before you buy.

Summary

Twenty-four laser-cut bins in a rack, each with an LED. Choose a kit (say, a sensor kit for a class) and the bins light up in order with the quantity on an OLED; press 'done' to advance. Recipes are edited from a phone.

What it teaches

Laser-cut a modular bin rack, address WS2812 LEDs per bin, build a phone web page where you enter or pick a kit recipe, and confirm each pick with a button so the next bin lights up. Measure time and error rate with and without lights.

Topics it touches, in Fab Academy-style terms:

  • Computer-controlled cutting
  • Output devices
  • Input devices
  • Interface and application programming
  • Networking and communications
