Weigh-and-Price Helper Scale for Home Micro-Businesses

  • intermediate
  • 6-8 hours

SDG 1 · No Poverty

A repairable, low-cost scale helps home food and craft sellers portion, cost and price goods consistently, protecting thin margins.

Safety notes

Read first. Not legal-for-trade. In India, scales used to sell goods must be verified and stamped under the Legal Metrology Act. Use this one for portioning, recipes and costing only.

  • Not legal-for-trade: in India, weighing instruments used to sell goods must be verified and stamped under the Legal Metrology Act. Use this for portioning, recipes and costing only.
  • Do not overload the cell past its rating; add a mechanical stop under the platform.

Draft and unofficial, not affiliated with Fab Academy or the United Nations. Check prices, GST and parts before you buy.

Summary

A kitchen-sized scale with a laser-cut body: weigh an ingredient or finished pack, enter a price per kg with buttons, and read the price and batch cost on screen. Aimed at home bakers, pickle makers and craft sellers who need consistent portions and costing.

What it teaches

Read a strain-gauge load cell through an HX711 24-bit ADC, calibrate with known weights, filter noisy readings, and build a tare / unit-price / batch-cost interface on an OLED. Design a laser-cut cantilever mount that keeps the load cell in bending only.

Topics it touches, in Fab Academy-style terms:

  • Input devices
  • Computer-controlled cutting
  • Embedded programming
  • Mechanical design
