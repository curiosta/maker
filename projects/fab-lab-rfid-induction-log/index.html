RFID Machine-Induction Check-in for Fab Lab Tools

  • intermediate
  • 6-8 hours

SDG 8 · Decent Work and Economic Growth

Supports safe working environments (target 8.8): only people inducted on a machine are shown as cleared, and usage hours are logged for fair cost sharing.

Safety notes

  • Information only: do not wire this version to switch mains power to a machine. Any interlock must be designed with a qualified electrician and must never bypass the machine's own e-stop.
  • Card UIDs (e.g. MIFARE Classic) can be copied; treat this as a training record, not access security.

Draft and unofficial, not affiliated with Fab Academy or the United Nations. Check prices, GST and parts before you buy.

Summary

A laser-cut wall unit beside a machine: tap your card, it shows whether you are inducted on that tool and starts a session timer; tap again to log off. Lab staff manage the allow-list from a web page.

What it teaches

Read 13.56 MHz cards over SPI, keep an allow-list per machine, show a clear green/red status, log sessions to a spreadsheet or MQTT, and think through privacy (store card IDs, not names) and the limits of card UIDs as identity.

Topics it touches, in Fab Academy-style terms:

  • Input devices
  • Output devices
  • Embedded programming
  • Networking and communications
  • Computer-controlled cutting
