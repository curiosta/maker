CNC-Milled Flat-Pack Street Vendor Stall

  • intermediate
  • 2-3 days (design, two sheets of milling, assembly)

SDG 1 · No Poverty

A sturdy, repairable stall a carpenter can recut from open files helps street vendors work from a better, movable setup without costly fabricated carts.

Safety notes

  • CNC router: secure the sheet with screws or clamps, wear eye and ear protection and a dust mask, keep hands away from the spindle, and stay at the machine with the e-stop in reach.
  • Check the casters' rated load against goods plus counter weight, and lock them before trading; keep the stall's centre of gravity low.

Draft and unofficial, not affiliated with Fab Academy or the United Nations. Check prices, GST and parts before you buy.

Summary

A stall that bolts together from two plywood sheets with cross-dowel joints and lockable casters: counter, shelf and awning frame. It packs flat for overnight storage and every part can be recut from the shared files.

What it teaches

Design big: parametric press-fit and knock-down joints for 18 mm plywood, dog-bone fillets for router-cut inside corners, nesting parts on a full sheet, CAM with tabs and safe feeds, and co-designing dimensions with a real vendor.

Topics it touches, in Fab Academy-style terms:

  • Computer-controlled machining
  • Computer-aided design
  • Mechanical design
  • Project management
