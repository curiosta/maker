Cool-Roof Experiment: Twin Laser-Cut Model Houses

  • beginner
  • 4-6 hours + a sunny day of logging

SDG 13 · Climate Action

Measures how a light-coloured roof keeps a building cooler in the sun, a simple, local adaptation to rising heat.

Safety notes

  • Laser cutter: never leave it running unattended, keep the lid closed and extraction on, and only cut materials your lab approves (no PVC or vinyl).
  • Sun and heat: dark surfaces get hot; handle with care and keep the electronics shaded.

Draft and unofficial, not affiliated with Fab Academy or the United Nations. Check prices, GST and parts before you buy.

Summary

Two small MDF houses side by side in the sun: one roof painted white or lime-washed, the other dark. An RP2040 logs both indoor temperatures every minute and the OLED shows the live difference.

What it teaches

Design identical press-fit model houses, run a controlled experiment (only roof colour differs), log two 1-Wire temperature probes plus ambient, and plot the difference to draw an evidence-based conclusion.

Topics it touches, in Fab Academy-style terms:

  • Computer-controlled cutting
  • Computer-aided design
  • Input devices
  • Embedded programming
  • Interface and application programming
