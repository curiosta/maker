Compost Health Probe (Temperature + Moisture, Low-Power)

  • beginner
  • 5-7 hours

SDG 12 · Responsible Consumption and Production

Helps a home, school or campus compost kitchen waste well, reducing waste sent to landfill (target 12.5).

Safety notes

  • LiPo: use a cell with a protection circuit, check polarity before connecting to the XIAO BAT pads, never puncture, crush or short it, and stop using a swollen cell. Keep the battery and board in a sealed housing outside the heap; only the probes go in.
  • Compost can carry pathogens: wear gloves and wash hands after handling.

Draft and unofficial, not affiliated with Fab Academy or the United Nations. Check prices, GST and parts before you buy.

Summary

Push the steel probe into the heap; every 15-30 minutes the node wakes, reads core temperature and moisture, sends them to a dashboard and sleeps. It tells you when to turn the pile or add water.

What it teaches

Read a 1-Wire temperature probe and a capacitive moisture sensor, use deep sleep to run for weeks on a LiPo, report over Wi-Fi, and interpret the hot-composting temperature curve (warm-up, hot phase, cooling).

Topics it touches, in Fab Academy-style terms:

  • Input devices
  • Embedded programming
  • Networking and communications
  • 3D scanning and printing
