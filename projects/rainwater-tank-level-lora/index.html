Rainwater Tank Level Monitor over LoRa (865 MHz)

  • intermediate
  • 10-14 hours

SDG 6 · Clean Water and Sanitation

Knowing how much harvested rainwater is in a rooftop or community tank helps households and schools plan use and avoid overflow.

Safety notes

  • Work on roofs and tank lids only with someone present and a secure footing; never lean into tanks.
  • India: use the licence-exempt 865-867 MHz band (WPC de-licensed it for low-power devices within the power limits in its notification); keep the module's default low power, use the IN865 settings, and buy modules whose seller states WPC ETA compliance. Never use 433 or 915 MHz modules.
  • 18650 cells: use protected cells, set any boost/buck output with a multimeter before connecting the board, never short the cell, and do not leave charging unattended.
  • Keep electronics outside the tank and drinking water uncontaminated: only the sealed sensor faces the water, nothing enters it.

Draft and unofficial, not affiliated with Fab Academy or the United Nations. Check prices, GST and parts before you buy.

Summary

A node on the tank lid reads the water surface distance every 15 minutes and sends the level to a second XIAO indoors with an OLED showing litres and a 7-day trend. No Wi-Fi needed at the roof.

What it teaches

Measure water level with a sealed ultrasonic sensor from the tank lid, convert distance to litres from tank geometry, send compact LoRa packets to an indoor receiver, and run on a solar-charged cell with deep sleep.

Topics it touches, in Fab Academy-style terms:

  • Input devices
  • Networking and communications
  • Embedded programming
  • 3D scanning and printing
  • System integration
