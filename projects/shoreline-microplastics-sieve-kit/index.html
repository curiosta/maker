Shoreline Microplastics Sieve Kit with Phone Microscope Stand

  • beginner
  • 4-6 hours + field sampling

SDG 14 · Life Below Water

Lets students and volunteer groups sample and count microplastics on beaches and riverbanks, building local evidence on marine litter (target 14.1).

Safety notes

  • Water and tides: never sample alone, check tide times, stay out of surf and away from slippery rocks.
  • Wear gloves: shorelines hide glass, needles and sharp debris. Leave shells and living creatures where they are.
  • Laser cutter: never leave it running unattended, keep the lid closed and extraction on, and only cut materials your lab approves (no PVC or vinyl).

Draft and unofficial, not affiliated with Fab Academy or the United Nations. Check prices, GST and parts before you buy.

Summary

Mark out a quadrat, scoop the top layer of sand, sieve it, float it in salt water and count the pieces between 1 and 5 mm under a phone macro lens. Record results the same way each time so sites and seasons can be compared.

What it teaches

Design a nesting laser-cut sieve stack (5 mm and 1 mm stainless mesh), a sampling quadrat and a phone macro stand; use a saturated salt solution to float many common plastics out of sand, then count and photograph particles with a consistent method.

Topics it touches, in Fab Academy-style terms:

  • Computer-controlled cutting
  • Computer-aided design
  • 3D scanning and printing
  • Molding and casting (optional)
