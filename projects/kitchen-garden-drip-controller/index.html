Soil-Moisture Drip Irrigation Controller (Milled MOSFET Board)

  • intermediate
  • 8-10 hours

SDG 2 · Zero Hunger

Waters a kitchen garden or small plot only when the soil is actually dry, supporting steadier home food growing with less water.

Safety notes

  • Water and electronics: mount the board and adapter above and away from the water tank in a closed case; run cables with a drip loop.
  • Power only from a certified 5 V USB adapter. Do not build or modify a mains supply.
  • Run the pump only when submerged, and add a maximum run-time limit in firmware so a failed sensor cannot empty the tank.

Draft and unofficial, not affiliated with Fab Academy or the United Nations. Check prices, GST and parts before you buy.

Summary

A XIAO ESP32-C3 reads soil moisture every few minutes; below your threshold it runs a small 5 V pump for a set number of seconds through drip tubing, then waits. A web page shows the moisture trend and lets you change thresholds.

What it teaches

Calibrate a capacitive soil sensor (dry air vs. wet soil), design and mill a low-side MOSFET switch with gate resistor, pull-down and flyback diode, add hysteresis so the pump does not chatter, and log moisture over Wi-Fi with deep sleep between readings.

Topics it touches, in Fab Academy-style terms:

  • Electronics design
  • Electronics production
  • Input devices
  • Output devices
  • Embedded programming
  • Networking and communications
