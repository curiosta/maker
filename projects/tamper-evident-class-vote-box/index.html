Tamper-Evident Electronic Vote Box for Clubs and Classrooms

  • intermediate
  • 8-10 hours

SDG 16 · Peace, Justice and Strong Institutions

Explores how to design for trust in group decisions: one vote per voter, a tamper flag and an auditable log, in the spirit of participatory decision-making (target 16.7).

Safety notes

Read first. For clubs, classrooms and community groups only. It is not a voting machine for public elections.

  • For clubs, classrooms and community groups only. It is not, and should not be presented as, a voting machine for public elections.

Draft and unofficial, not affiliated with Fab Academy or the United Nations. Check prices, GST and parts before you buy.

Summary

A laser-cut box with up to four choices: the chair enables one vote at a time, the voter presses a choice and gets a light and beep, and results appear only when the session is closed. Opening the lid mid-session is flagged on the results screen.

What it teaches

Mill a board with choice buttons, a chair's enable button and a lid switch; implement a one-vote-per-enable state machine, a hash-chained vote log in flash, and a tamper flag if the lid opens mid-session. Discuss what this does and does not prove.

Topics it touches, in Fab Academy-style terms:

  • Electronics design
  • Electronics production
  • Computer-controlled cutting
  • Input devices
  • Output devices
  • Embedded programming
