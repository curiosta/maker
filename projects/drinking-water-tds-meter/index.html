Drinking-Water TDS and Temperature Meter

  • beginner
  • 4-6 hours

SDG 6 · Clean Water and Sanitation

Lets households and schools compare water sources and check whether a filter or RO unit is still reducing dissolved solids.

Safety notes

  • TDS is not a safety test: it does not detect bacteria, arsenic, fluoride, nitrate or other contaminants. Never use it to decide water is safe to drink; use an accredited lab test for that.
  • Do not drink a sample after the probes have been in it. Keep the board and USB side dry.

Draft and unofficial, not affiliated with Fab Academy or the United Nations. Check prices, GST and parts before you buy.

Summary

Dip the two probes in a sample: the OLED shows TDS (ppm) and water temperature, and logs readings over USB serial so you can compare tap, borewell, filtered and RO water.

What it teaches

Read an analog conductivity-based TDS probe, apply temperature compensation from a 1-Wire DS18B20, calibrate against a reference solution, and understand what TDS can and cannot tell you about water.

Topics it touches, in Fab Academy-style terms:

  • Input devices
  • Embedded programming
  • 3D scanning and printing (optional probe holder)
