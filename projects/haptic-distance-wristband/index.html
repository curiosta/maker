Haptic Distance Wristband (ToF Sensor, Vibration Feedback)

  • intermediate
  • 8-10 hours

SDG 10 · Reduced Inequalities

A low-cost, repairable feedback aid prototype, co-designed with people with low vision, supports inclusion of persons with disabilities (target 10.2).

Safety notes

  • LiPo: use a cell with a protection circuit, check polarity before connecting to the XIAO BAT pads, never puncture, crush or short it, and stop using a swollen cell.
  • Prototype only: it does not replace a white cane or orientation and mobility training. ToF sensors can miss glass, very dark surfaces and objects outside the narrow beam (e.g. drop-offs).

Draft and unofficial, not affiliated with Fab Academy or the United Nations. Check prices, GST and parts before you buy.

Summary

A wrist-worn unit that vibrates faster as an object gets closer (up to about 3-4 m indoors). Built on a small milled board with a LiPo, and designed with users rather than for them.

What it teaches

Read a VL53L1X time-of-flight sensor over I2C, map distance to vibration intensity and rhythm with PWM through a MOSFET and flyback diode, design a wearable enclosure, and run user tests to tune the feedback.

Topics it touches, in Fab Academy-style terms:

  • Electronics design
  • Electronics production
  • Input devices
  • Output devices
  • 3D scanning and printing
  • Embedded programming
