Ultrasonic Radar Scanner (RP2040 + Servo + OLED)

  • beginner
  • 4-6 hours

Misc

A general sensing and servo starter project; it has no honest SDG fit without turning it into a different build.

Safety notes

No project-specific safety notes have been written for this draft yet. Soldering, laser cutting and milling each carry their own hazards, so follow your lab's safety rules and ask someone experienced if unsure.

Draft and unofficial, not affiliated with Fab Academy or the United Nations. Check prices, GST and parts before you buy.

Summary

A servo sweeps an HC-SR04 across 180 degrees; XIAO RP2040 plots distance vs angle as a radar-style sweep on an OLED (and over USB serial for a PC plot). Includes a laser-cut mount.

What it teaches

Timing-based distance sensing, driving a servo with PWM, level-shifting a 5V echo line to a 3.3V MCU with a resistor divider, and drawing a polar sweep on an OLED. Pairs a laser-cut pan bracket with MicroPython on RP2040.

Topics it touches, in Fab Academy-style terms:

  • Input devices
  • Output devices
  • Embedded programming
  • Mechanical design
  • Computer-controlled cutting
