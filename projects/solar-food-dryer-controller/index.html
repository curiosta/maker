Solar Food Dryer with Fan and Temperature Control

  • intermediate
  • 10-12 hours

SDG 2 · Zero Hunger

Drying surplus fruit, vegetables and herbs with sunlight cuts post-harvest loss and keeps food longer without grid power.

Safety notes

  • Food safety: use food-grade stainless mesh only (no galvanised or painted mesh), keep insects out with fine screens, and follow tested drying guidance for each food.
  • Heat: collector surfaces get hot in full sun; acrylic glazing softens if overheated, so vent the box.
  • 18650 cells: use protected cells, set any boost/buck output with a multimeter before connecting the board, never short the cell, and do not leave charging unattended.

Draft and unofficial, not affiliated with Fab Academy or the United Nations. Check prices, GST and parts before you buy.

Summary

A tilted collector heats air that rises through food-grade mesh trays. An RP2040 reads tray temperature and humidity and runs a fan to vent moisture and cap temperature, logging a drying curve so you can tell when a batch is done.

What it teaches

Design a laser-cut plywood box dryer with a clear glazing and airflow path, measure air temperature and humidity at the trays, and run a solar-charged fan through a MOSFET with hysteresis to keep drying air within a safe band.

Topics it touches, in Fab Academy-style terms:

  • Computer-controlled cutting
  • Input devices
  • Output devices
  • Electronics production
  • Embedded programming
