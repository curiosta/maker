Classroom CO2 Ventilation Monitor (Traffic-Light Display)

  • beginner
  • 3-5 hours

SDG 3 · Good Health and Well-being

Shows when a crowded room needs fresh air, a simple prompt for better ventilation and healthier indoor spaces.

Safety notes

  • CO2 is a ventilation indicator, not a measure of infection risk or of other pollutants. It is not a CO alarm: never use it to detect carbon monoxide.

Draft and unofficial, not affiliated with Fab Academy or the United Nations. Check prices, GST and parts before you buy.

Summary

A laser-cut wall unit with a CO2 readout and an LED bar: green when ventilation looks fine, amber and red as CO2 builds up. Outdoor air is roughly 420 ppm; many guidelines use around 800-1000 ppm as a prompt to ventilate.

What it teaches

Read a true photoacoustic CO2 sensor (SCD40) over I2C, understand why CO2 is a proxy for how much exhaled air is in a room, map readings to a traffic-light LED bar, and log a school day to see the effect of opening windows.

Topics it touches, in Fab Academy-style terms:

  • Input devices
  • Output devices
  • Embedded programming
  • Computer-controlled cutting
