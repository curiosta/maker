Street Noise Level Meter (I2S Microphone, dB Logging)

  • intermediate
  • 6-8 hours

SDG 11 · Sustainable Cities and Communities

Maps noise exposure around a school, market or junction, one of the urban environmental impacts that target 11.6 asks cities to reduce.

Safety notes

  • Indicative only: an uncalibrated MEMS meter is not a Class 1/2 sound level meter and its readings cannot be used for official noise-rule complaints.
  • Record levels, not audio, to respect privacy.

Draft and unofficial, not affiliated with Fab Academy or the United Nations. Check prices, GST and parts before you buy.

Summary

An ESP32 with an INMP441 microphone shows the current and peak sound level on an OLED and an LED bar, and logs readings to a dashboard so you can compare places and times of day.

What it teaches

Capture audio over I2S, compute RMS level in dBFS, apply an approximate A-weighting, calibrate the offset against a reference meter, and log time-stamped readings over Wi-Fi with an honest uncertainty note.

Topics it touches, in Fab Academy-style terms:

  • Input devices
  • Output devices
  • Embedded programming
  • Networking and communications
  • Computer-controlled cutting (optional case)
