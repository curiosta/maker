Laser-Cut Truss Bridge with Tilt and Vibration Sensing

  • intermediate
  • 6-8 hours

SDG 9 · Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure

A hands-on model of structural health monitoring, the kind of sensing that helps keep bridges and other infrastructure resilient (target 9.1).

Safety notes

  • Load tests: wear eye protection, hang weights in a tray kept low over the bench, and keep feet clear; MDF snaps suddenly.

Draft and unofficial, not affiliated with Fab Academy or the United Nations. Check prices, GST and parts before you buy.

Summary

Cut a 40-50 cm MDF truss bridge, mount an accelerometer at mid-span and stream tilt and vibration to a laptop plot. Add load step by step and watch stiffness and frequency change before failure.

What it teaches

Design a parametric press-fit truss (kerf compensation, joint design), load-test it, and read an MPU-6050 to measure deflection angle and natural frequency (FFT of a tap test). Compare designs by stiffness per gram.

Topics it touches, in Fab Academy-style terms:

  • Computer-aided design
  • Computer-controlled cutting
  • Input devices
  • Embedded programming
  • Interface and application programming
