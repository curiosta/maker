Cast Epoxy Glow Lamp (3D-Printed Master, Silicone Mould, Wi-Fi LEDs)

  • advanced
  • 12-16 hours over 2-3 days (cure times)

Draft and unofficial, not affiliated with Fab Academy. Check prices, GST and parts before you buy.

Summary

A desktop mood lamp: the diffuser is cast in a silicone mould taken from your own 3D-printed master. A 12-LED ring and ESP32-C3 sit in the base; a button cycles modes and a small web page sets the colour over Wi-Fi.

What it teaches

Design a diffuser dome with a cavity for the electronics, print the master, make a two-part silicone mould (parting line, pour/vent design, release), cast in clear epoxy, finish (sand to frost), then drive a WS2812 ring from XIAO ESP32-C3 with a Wi-Fi colour picker page.

Topics it touches, in Fab Academy-style terms:

  • 3D scanning and printing
  • Molding and casting
  • Output devices
  • Embedded programming
  • Networking and communications
  • Computer-aided design

Safety notes

  • Epoxy and silicone: work ventilated, wear nitrile gloves and eye protection, follow the supplier's mix ratio by weight.
  • Do not cast electronics into epoxy; keep the LED ring in a removable base (heat and repairability).
