Free Menstrual Pad Dispenser with Low-Stock Alert for Schools

  • intermediate
  • 10-12 hours

SDG 5 · Gender Equality

Reliable, dignified access to menstrual products in school toilets supports girls' health, comfort and attendance.

Safety notes

  • Privacy: no camera, no user identification and no per-person logs; only stock counts leave the device.
  • Use only a certified external adapter; do not open, build or modify mains supplies. Mount the adapter and electronics away from taps and splash zones.
  • Keep fingers clear of the servo pusher while testing.

Draft and unofficial, not affiliated with Fab Academy or the United Nations. Check prices, GST and parts before you buy.

Summary

A wall-mounted, button-operated dispenser: one press releases one pad, free of charge. When stock runs low, staff get a notification; the unit counts dispenses only, with no camera and no record of who used it.

What it teaches

Design a laser-cut gravity magazine with a servo-driven pusher, detect an empty magazine with an IR sensor, and send a low-stock message to staff over Wi-Fi so refills happen before it runs out. Co-design placement and use with students.

Topics it touches, in Fab Academy-style terms:

  • Computer-controlled cutting
  • Mechanical design
  • Input devices
  • Output devices
  • Networking and communications
