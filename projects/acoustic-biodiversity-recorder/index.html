Acoustic Biodiversity Recorder (Scheduled Dawn Chorus Logger)

  • advanced
  • 14-18 hours

SDG 15 · Life on Land

Records birds, frogs and insects on a schedule so a campus or village can track how biodiversity changes over seasons (target 15.5).

Safety notes

  • Privacy: microphones can capture people talking. Place it away from paths and homes, put up a small notice, and delete recordings containing voices.
  • 18650 cells: use protected cells, set any boost/buck output with a multimeter before connecting the board, never short the cell, and do not leave charging unattended.
  • In forests or protected areas, get permission from the forest department first; do not disturb nests or roosts.

Draft and unofficial, not affiliated with Fab Academy or the United Nations. Check prices, GST and parts before you buy.

Summary

Strap it to a tree for a week: it wakes at dawn and dusk, records a few minutes each time, and sleeps. Back at the lab, look through spectrograms to identify species and compare sites.

What it teaches

Capture I2S audio and write WAV files to microSD on a schedule (dawn and dusk), budget power across days with deep sleep, design a weatherproof printed housing with an acoustic port, and analyse recordings with free tools such as spectrograms and BirdNET.

Topics it touches, in Fab Academy-style terms:

  • Input devices
  • Embedded programming
  • Electronics production
  • 3D scanning and printing
  • System integration
