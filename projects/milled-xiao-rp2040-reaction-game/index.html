Milled Reaction-Time Trainer Board (XIAO RP2040)

  • intermediate
  • 8-10 hours

SDG 3 · Good Health and Well-being

Turns the game into a reaction-time and hand-eye coordination trainer that logs your times, encouraging short, playful movement and focus breaks.

Safety notes

  • Wellness and play only: it is not a medical or diagnostic device, and reaction times vary with sleep, time of day and practice.
  • Soldering: ventilate, use a stand, and keep fingers clear of the tip.

Draft and unofficial, not affiliated with Fab Academy or the United Nations. Check prices, GST and parts before you buy.

Summary

A custom carrier PCB you design and mill: XIAO RP2040 socketed in female headers, 4 arcade-style tactile buttons, an 8-LED WS2812 bar, passive buzzer and OLED. Light a random LED, press the matching button fast; the board logs each reaction time in milliseconds and shows your session best, average and day-to-day trend.

What it teaches

Full board loop: schematic and layout in KiCad (single-sided, jumper-friendly), mill on a desktop CNC/PCB mill, solder through-hole parts, then program a game with buttons, WS2812 LEDs, buzzer and OLED score display. Covers DRC, tool-path generation, and debugging a fresh board.

Topics it touches, in Fab Academy-style terms:

  • Electronics design
  • Electronics production
  • Embedded programming
  • Input devices
  • Output devices
