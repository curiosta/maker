How it works

  1. Open the tutor

    Use the button above, or the "Ask the tutor" button at the bottom of any page.

  2. Connect your own OpenRouter account

    You sign in with OpenRouter and the tutor uses your own key. By default the key stays in this browser tab and is cleared when you close it or disconnect. You can choose to remember it on this device for up to 7 days; only do that on a device you own.

  3. Ask

    The default model is one of OpenRouter's free ones, and you can change it. Free-model availability and limits are set by OpenRouter, not by this site.

What to expect

  • Answers come from an AI model reading pages on this site. It can be wrong, so check anything important on fabacademy.org.
  • It will not do your assignments for you. It coaches, explains and reviews.
  • This site has no server and stores nothing about you. Your questions go from your browser to OpenRouter and the model provider you pick, under their terms and privacy policies.
  • Where something is not covered on the official Fab Academy pages or on this site, the tutor should say so rather than guess.

Some things to try

  • "I'm coming back after a break. Where should I start?"
  • "What does the electronics design module ask for?"
  • "What would exceptional embedded programming work look like?"
